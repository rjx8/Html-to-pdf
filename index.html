Unit 1: Big Changes
Complete Study Summary - Mega Goal 1

Unit Introduction

Unit 1 "Big Changes" focuses on major global changes that have shaped our modern world and how these changes have affected people's lives and societies. The unit provides a comprehensive overview of important historical events and technological developments that changed human history.

Through this unit, students learn to express opinions, discuss global issues, and use different grammatical tenses to describe past and current events.

Main Topics
Key Focus Areas:
  • Major Historical Changes: Unification of Saudi Arabia, Establishment of UAE, Space Race
  • Communication Revolution: Technological development and its social impact
  • Global Issues: Global warming, poverty, unemployment, etc.
  • Grammar: Different tenses and their uses
  • Language Skills: Listening, Speaking, Reading, Writing
Learning Objectives
By the end of this unit, you will be able to:
  • Discuss past world events and their present effects
  • Talk about global issues and express your opinions
  • Use different verb tenses correctly
  • Understand and use new vocabulary related to changes
  • Write about important events and their impacts
  • Conduct interviews and present findings

Grammar: Verb Tenses
Simple Present Tense

Usage: Facts, permanent situations, habits and routines

Form: base form (he/she/it + -s)

Examples:

  • The earth goes around the sun
  • I work in an office every day
  • Water boils at 100°C
  • She speaks three languages
Time Expressions: always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day/week/month
Present Progressive/Continuous Tense

Usage: Actions happening now, temporary situations, developing situations

Form: am/is/are + verb-ing

Examples:

  • I am studying English now
  • She is working in Dubai this year
  • They are watching TV at the moment
  • The number of immigrants is increasing
Time Expressions: now, at the moment, currently, these days, this year/month
Simple Past Tense

Usage: Completed actions in the past, specific time in the past

Form: verb + -ed (or irregular past form)

Examples:

  • I visited Paris last year
  • She worked in a bank in 2020
  • They launched the satellite in 1957
  • He was born in Berlin
Time Expressions: yesterday, last week/month/year, ago, in 1990, when I was young

More Grammar Tenses
Present Perfect Tense

Usage: Actions that started in the past and continue to present, experiences

Form: have/has + past participle

Examples:

  • I have lived here for five years
  • She has worked here since 2020
  • They have known each other for a long time
  • He has visited three countries
Time Expressions: for, since, already, yet, just, ever, never, recently
Past Progressive Tense

Usage: Actions in progress at specific time in past, interrupted actions

Form: was/were + verb-ing

Examples:

  • I was studying when you called
  • They were watching TV at 8 PM yesterday
  • While I was cooking, he was cleaning
  • She was sleeping when the phone rang
Pattern: Past Progressive + when + Simple Past
Tense Comparison Table
Tense Usage Example Time Words
Simple Present Facts, habits I work every day always, usually
Present Continuous Now, temporary I am working now now, at the moment
Simple Past Completed past I worked yesterday yesterday, last week
Present Perfect Past to present I have worked for 2 years for, since, already
Past Continuous Past in progress I was working at 8pm while, when

Writing: How the Internet Changed the World

The Internet started in 1969 and has revolutionized communication, information access, and education. Key changes include:

Internet Impact Areas:
  • Communication: Email, instant messaging, video calls
  • Information: Easy access through search engines
  • Education: Online learning and academic qualifications
  • Social Networks: Connect with friends worldwide
Writing Practice
Writing Task: Write about an important event that brought major changes to your country.

Include:
  • What was the event?
  • When and where did it happen?
  • What impacts did it have?
  • Was the impact positive or negative?
Useful Phrases: "One important event that changed my country was...", "This event happened in...", "As a result...", "The main impacts were...", "In my opinion..."
Project: Personal Interview

Objective: Interview someone who has lived in another country and document their experience.

Project Steps:
  1. Find a person to interview (relative or friend)
  2. Prepare interview questions
  3. Conduct the interview and take notes
  4. Organize information in a chart
  5. Present your findings to the class
Sample Interview Questions:
  • Where were you born and where did you grow up?
  • When did you move to another country?
  • What challenges did you face?
  • What did you learn from the experience?
  • How has living abroad changed you?
  • Do you still have relatives in your home country?
